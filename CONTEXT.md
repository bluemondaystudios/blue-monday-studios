1. COMPANY IDENTITY

1.1 What is Blue Monday Studios?

Short description:

We are a business helping businesses build their digital infrastructure. We help businesses build their entire digital pipeline that will influence their sales, communication and CRM dynamics.

30-second explanation:

Do you have a search-engine-optimized Google Business Profile? We are here. Do you think your business can benefit from a website or web app? We are here.

---

1.2 Core Business Concept

Blue Monday Studios provides:

A way for real, operating small businesses to become digitally reachable. A business can have a great product and still lose opportunities because customers cannot find it, trust it, contact it, or understand what it offers. Only around 25% of small businesses in South Africa have a properly set up digital infrastructure (Source: FNB App Academy).

We do this by:

Connecting the digital pieces that a business actually needs, such as a website, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, professional email, and visual identity, so they work as one connected system instead of separate, isolated assets. Where a business needs more, we also build custom digital systems (web applications, dashboards, workflows, automation).

Our primary customers are:

Small and medium sized businesses in South Africa that are already operating with real customers, but are missing some or all of the digital layer that would let those customers find, trust, and reach them.

Working Philosophy

Edward approaches problems through systems thinking: understanding how the parts of a business, and the parts of a piece of software, connect and depend on one another, rather than treating each part in isolation. This is the same lens applied to clients (a website is not evaluated on its own, but as one node in the client's full digital infrastructure) and to internal architecture (a feature is not built in isolation, but as part of a coherent system). 3. BUSINESS PHILOSOPHY

3.1 Core Belief

Digital transformation should not be a privilege reserved for big business. As South Africa moves deeper into the Fourth Industrial Revolution, small businesses need practical, accessible ways to participate in the digital economy, not enterprise-scale solutions built for a different kind of company.

---

3.2 Digital Infrastructure Philosophy

Blue Monday Studios uses the concept of digital infrastructure scaffolding.

Meaning

The layers of a business's digital presence (website, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, email, visual identity, and so on) function as scaffolding that supports how customers discover, trust, and reach the business. Removing or neglecting any one piece weakens the structure as a whole. The goal is not to sell individual tools, but to build and maintain the scaffolding that holds a business's digital presence together.

Practical Interpretation

A client's digital infrastructure may include:

- Website
- Google Business Profile
- WhatsApp Business
- Email
- Instagram
- Facebook
- Other relevant social platforms
- Search visibility
- Digital communication systems
- Online booking systems
- Custom software
- [Other]

Blue Monday does not automatically recommend every platform.

Instead:

«We observe the business, understand its customers and operating environment, and determine which digital infrastructure is actually relevant.»

7.4 Proposal

---

7.5 Implementation

Edward builds the work with minimal back-and-forth during the build itself. The client generally sees the result once it is essentially finished, rather than through iterative check-ins along the way.

---

7.6 Deployment

The finished build is deployed directly, the client sees it live rather than reviewing a separate staged/approval step beforehand. Web applications deploy to Railway; static sites deploy to GitHub Pages (this repository's own site deploys via a GitHub Actions workflow at .github/workflows/deploy.yml).

---

7.7 Handover

At completion, the client receives written documentation: a short guide covering what was set up and how to use it. Credential handover, walkthrough/training calls, and a default ongoing-support window are not yet standard parts of handover; support beyond delivery is currently handled case by case rather than included by default (see Section 19 for the open question on formalizing this).
